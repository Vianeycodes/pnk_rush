FAQ

      
Good to know

    

    

      
What do you need to get started?
A short brief: the product, what you want viewers to know, deliverables, timeline and where the content will be used. The builder above covers most of it.

      
Can we use the content in ads?
Yes. Usage rights and paid ad licensing are quoted per project based on channels and length of use. Add "Paid ad usage" in the builder to include it.

      
Do you work with gifted products?
Gifted collabs are considered when the product is a good fit for the car and the audience. Deliverables are agreed in writing before shipping.

      
Are partnerships disclosed?
Always. Paid, gifted and affiliate content is clearly labeled, including Instagram's Paid partnership label where it applies.

      
How do rates work?
Rates depend on deliverables, usage rights, exclusivity and timeline. Send a request with the builder and you'll get a rate card with current Insights.
